How this works

Every prompt hides the Hangul. Letters gives you the sound of a letter (e.g. "g / k") and asks for the syllable block used to teach it (e.g. 가). Words gives you the English meaning and asks for the whole word, one syllable per box.

  • Write first, from memory. Tap Show guide only if you're stuck — it traces a faint copy of the answer inside the box for you to draw over.
  • Tap Reveal answer to check your own writing against the real word, then mark ✓ Got it or ↻ Again honestly. Missed ones come back around sooner.
  • On iPad, draw with Apple Pencil for the most natural line — finger and mouse work too.
  • Progress is saved on this device only, separately for Letters and Words.
